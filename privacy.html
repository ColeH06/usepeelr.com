Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Peelr collects, uses, and protects information when you use our price-tracking service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect product URLs you paste, your browser’s notification token, basic device data, and error logs. Peelr does not require account creation.

2. How Information Is Used

We use your information to send price drop alerts, maintain the service, prevent abuse, and comply with affiliate requirements. We do not collect, redistribute, and/or sell your personal data.

3. Sharing Information

We may share limited necessary data with affiliate networks, analytics providers, or retailers when you click a link.

4. Cookies

Peelr uses basic cookies for functionality and analytics. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.
